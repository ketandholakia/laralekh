## Retained CA-review / fail-closed boundaries

- **GSTIN portal registration status (`gstin_portal_existence_unverified`)**: `GstinValidator` deterministically validates GSTIN structure, state code, and Mod-36 checksum (failing closed on invalid GSTINs). Live portal registration/cancellation status requires external GSP/IRP API credentials, which are excluded by design (DEC-005). Therefore, portal existence is explicitly retained as a non-blocking CA-review warning.
- **Transaction-level place-of-supply legal treatment**: Active 2-digit State/UT master validation is enforced, but statutory determination under IGST Act Sections 10–13 (e.g., bill-to-ship-to, immovable property, performance-based services) remains a CA-review responsibility.
- **Unreviewed purchase ITC lines (`itc_eligibility_pending_review`)**: Taxable purchase lines marked `review` (rather than `eligible` or `blocked`) emit a CA-review warning for Section 17(5) verification prior to filing.
- **Correction reporting-period convention**: Rule amendments, omissions, and corrections are assigned to the month of `reporting_date`, falling back to the correction date only where no reporting date exists. Ledger tie-out groups a posted correction voucher into the return/reporting period for the comparison, even if its accounting voucher date differs.
- **Historical cancellations outside LaraLekh**: Document-issued counts include recorded cancelled sales invoices; historical cancellations made outside this application are not inferable and require CA review.
- **GSTR-1 JSON export**: Remains blocked (`LL-P2-001`) pending vendoring of the official GST portal JSON schema into `docs/gst-schemas/`. CSV output is for review/import assistance only.

## GSTR-3B worksheet design

Rows are categorized for reconciliation with portal-populated 3B amounts. Output tax and eligible ITC are compared to `OUTPUT_*` / `INPUT_*` ledger credit/debit movements. Reverse-charge entries remain explicitly separate. The worksheet retains net payable at tax-head precision and applies `GstCalculator::roundStatutoryPayable` only to the combined statutory payable total; source transactions are not rounded to whole rupees.
