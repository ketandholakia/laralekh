## Fail-closed / not verified

- `config/gst_returns.php` marks GSTIN checksum validation, place-of-supply legal treatment, blocked-ITC statutory eligibility, and document-series document-nature mapping unverified. Tax rates are accepted only from effective-dated `TaxRate` records explicitly marked verified; rates are not guessed/defaulted. Place-of-supply codes must exist in the active local State master, but transaction-specific legal treatment remains for CA review.
- Rule amendments, omissions, and corrections are assigned to the month of `reporting_date`, falling back to the correction date only where no reporting date exists. Ledger tie-out groups a posted correction voucher into the return/reporting period for the comparison, even if its accounting voucher date differs. This is a return-period reconciliation convention, not a complete statutory deadline determination; CA review required.
- Document-issued counts now include recorded cancelled sales invoices; purchase bills are not part of the GSTR-1 Table 13 document-issued dataset. Historical cancellations made outside this application are not inferable and require migration/import or CA review. Credit/debit note cancellation and exact Table 13 series-range semantics remain for CA review.
- Official Table 13 manual specifically names “Invoices for outward supply”; the official CSV example contains that value. The accepted complete category enumeration and how multiple internal series map into ranges are not exposed in the fetched manual, so credit/debit note nature rows and other categories are marked unverified and block export until confirmed.
- The app has no durable AATO field, filing credentials, GSTR-2B source, GST portal HSN master, or JSON schema. CSV output is for review/import assistance only. No GSTR-1 JSON or filing is implemented.

## GSTR-3B worksheet design

Rows are categorized for reconciliation with portal-populated 3B amounts. Output tax and eligible ITC are compared to `OUTPUT_*` / `INPUT_*` ledger credit/debit movements. Reverse-charge entries remain explicitly separate. The worksheet retains net payable at tax-head precision and applies `GstCalculator::roundStatutoryPayable` only to the combined statutory payable total; source transactions are not rounded to whole rupees.
